Tips

Citrus(Lemon & Orange):

Tip: Choose heavy fruits for more juice.
Roll them on the counter before squeezing to get every drop!

Berries (Strawberry & Blueberry):

Tip: Look for deep red strawberries and "dusty" blue blueberries.
Don't wash them until you are ready to eat.

Tropical (Mango, Pineapple & Papaya):

Tip: A sweet smell at the stem or base means it's ripe.
If it's too hard, keep it in a paper bag for two days.

Storage Wisdom:

Keep fruits away from your vegetables to keep your greens fresh for a longer time!
