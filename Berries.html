Berries

Blueberries

Blueberries are small and sweet.
They are very healthy and full of vitamins.

Health Benefits of Blueberries:

  • 1- Improves memory and brain power.
  • 2- Protects the body with powerful antioxidants.

Strawberries

Strawberries are bright red and heart-shaped.
They taste amazing in desserts!

Health Benefits of Strawberries:

  • 1- Boosts the immune system with Vitamin C.
  • 2- Helps maintain healthy and glowing skin.
